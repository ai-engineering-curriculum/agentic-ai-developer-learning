## Overview

Entry rung (level 20) of the Agentic track and on-ramp to `agentic-ai-engineer-learning` (level 30). Covers LLM API fundamentals, prompt engineering and structured output, tool calling, retrieval basics, a first reason-act agent, and shipping an LLM app, followed by the production craft every LLM feature needs: streaming and async orchestration, model selection and cost control with prompt caching, and minimal golden-set regression checks.

Total commitment: **90 hours across 9 modules** + **50 hours across 3 projects** = **~140 hours**.
